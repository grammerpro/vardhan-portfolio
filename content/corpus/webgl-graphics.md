This is independent work rather than employment. Vardhan builds with Three.js and WebGL, including custom GLSL shaders. Chroma Loop is a shader-driven ribbon experiment, and this portfolio's hero is a generated structure rendered as line geometry with a vertex-shader assembly animation and depth-weighted opacity. He works with GSAP and ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography. He has not shipped WebGL in an enterprise production context.
